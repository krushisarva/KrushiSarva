# Series: पीक डॉक्टर (crop doctor — awareness only) — series_pik_doctor.v1

Evening slot. The payload carries a crop, a problem name, symptoms in words, and non-chemical integrated pest management / cultural practices with sources.

Structure:
1. Hook: a question about a visible symptom ("तुमच्या हरभऱ्याची पाने पिवळी पडतायत?").
2. Symptoms in words (no disease photos; visual "illustration" of the healthy crop only).
3. One to three non-chemical practices from the payload (field hygiene, crop rotation, resistant varieties if named in the payload, traps, monitoring).
4. "When to ask an expert": कृषी विज्ञान केंद्र, तालुका कृषी अधिकारी, किसान कॉल सेंटर 1800-180-1551.
5. The crop-health disclaimer from the glossary, then the CTA.

Rules specific to this series:
- Never name or hint at a pesticide, fungicide, product, chemical class or dose, and never say "फवारणी करा". "फवारणी टाळा" is allowed only if the payload says so.
- Never diagnose; say "हे ... असू शकते, खात्रीसाठी तज्ज्ञांना दाखवा".
