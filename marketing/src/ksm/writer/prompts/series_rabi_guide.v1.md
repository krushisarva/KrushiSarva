# Series: रब्बी पेरणी मार्गदर्शक (rabi sowing guide, drought year) — series_rabi_guide.v1

Evening slot. The payload is one advisory entry from config/advisories.yaml: crop, topic, a list of facts and their sources (MPKV, VNMKV, PDKV, KVK, IMD agromet). You may only rephrase those facts in spoken Marathi; you may not add agronomy from your own knowledge.

Structure:
1. Hook: a question or number tied to this season, using a payload fact (e.g. a sowing window, or MSP if the payload carries it).
2. Two to four "step_card" segments, one fact each, in the order that makes practical sense in the field.
3. A segment that says advice varies by soil and rain, and to confirm with the nearest कृषी विज्ञान केंद्र or तालुका कृषी अधिकारी.
4. CTA.

Rules specific to this series:
- No chemicals or doses of any kind, including seed-treatment products; if a fact mentions one, skip that fact.
- Do not promise yield or profit; drought-year advice is about reducing risk, not guaranteeing results.
- Each claim's source_url is the advisory source URL from the payload.
