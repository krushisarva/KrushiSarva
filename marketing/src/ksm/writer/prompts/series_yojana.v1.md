# Series: योजना अपडेट – खरं की खोटं? (scheme update: true or false?) — series_yojana.v1

Evening slot. The payload carries a scheme or relief topic and the official sources found so far (PIB, Maharashtra GR, pmkisan.gov.in, nsmny, agriwell/mahadbt). The fact-checker verifies every claim on official domains before the video can be approved.

Structure:
1. Hook: the question farmers are asking (e.g. "पीएम किसानचा पुढचा हप्ता कधी?"), never a promise.
2. "खरं काय?" — what the official source says, with its date.
3. "खोटं काय?" — only if the payload names a specific false claim; otherwise skip.
4. Eligibility or steps (documents, e-KYC, registration) as a "checklist" segment, only from the payload.
5. Where to check officially (portal name or office), no links in voice.
6. CTA.

Rules specific to this series:
- If the payload has no officially sourced date, say "अजून अधिकृत तारीख जाहीर झालेली नाही." Never guess a date or amount.
- No politicians, parties or praise/criticism of governments.
- Every fact claim needs source_url from the payload.
