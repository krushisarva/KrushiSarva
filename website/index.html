What we do

A farmer makes a dozen decisions a week. Each one needs a different app, a phone call or a trip to town.

KrushiSarva puts those decisions in one place and answers them in the farmer’s language.

Know what to do

Weather, crop guides, soil guidance and an AI assistant that answers farming questions by text or voice.

Catch problems early

A photo of a sick leaf returns the likely disease, how serious it is and a treatment plan the farmer can act on.

Buy, sell and rent nearby

Inputs from verified Krushi Seva Kendras (local farm-supply shops) serving the farmer’s area, plus livestock, tractors and labour listed by distance.

The product

Nine services, one sign-in

Each of these is built into the KrushiSarva farmer app.

Krushi AI assistant

Ask any farming question and get a clear, practical answer in your language.

Crop disease scan

Diagnosis, severity and treatment options from photos of the crop.

Voice in local languages

Speak the question and hear the answer. No typing needed.

AgriStore

Seeds, fertilisers, crop protection and tools from KYC-verified sellers, with online payment and delivery tracking.

Mandi prices

Daily mandi rates by commodity and market, with the highest, lowest and average price.

Weather for the field

Hourly and weekly forecast with advice on spraying and irrigation.

Livestock market

List and find cows, buffaloes, bullocks and more, then chat with the other farmer directly.

Machinery and labour on rent

Book a tractor, harvester or a labour team for the days it is needed.

My Farm

Log every activity from sowing to sale, and see what each crop cost and earned.

Also in the app: soil health guidance, government scheme information, state-wise crop guides and an input cost calculator.

How it is built

One backend behind three apps

Farmer, seller and admin

A farmer app and a seller app for Android, plus a web admin console, all running on one backend.

Verified sellers

Every Krushi Seva Kendra passes a KYC review by our team before it can sell.

Secure payments

Online payments run through Razorpay. Prices and totals are always calculated on our servers.

Privacy by design

OTP sign-in, explicit consent at sign-up, encryption of sensitive details and in-app account deletion.

Our goals

Where we are taking KrushiSarva

  1. Reach 100,000 farmers

    Our target is 100,000 registered farmers. We are building the platform to serve that number and to grow beyond it.

  2. Advice every farmer can use

    Give farmers crop guidance in their own language, by text, photo or voice.

  3. A fair, local market

    Connect farmers with verified input sellers, livestock buyers and machinery owners near them, with clear prices.

  4. Better decisions, better income

    Help farmers lose less crop to disease, sell at a better price and know what each season really earned.
