2. Permissions the apps ask for

  • Camera and photos — to photograph or pick images of crops, soil, products, animals, machinery and documents.
  • Microphone (farmer app) — only for AI voice chat.
  • Location (farmer app) — see "Precise location" above. You can refuse; type your PIN code instead.
  • Notifications — order, booking, message and safety alerts. You can switch them off in the app or in your phone settings.

3. Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what each task needs.

Other people on KrushiSarva

  • A seller receives the buyer's name, mobile number and delivery address for an order.
  • Your mobile number on an animal, machinery or labour listing can be revealed to a signed-in user who asks for it.
  • Your public listings, posts, comments and reviews are visible to other users under your name.

4. How we protect it

  • Aadhaar number, PAN, bank account details, GSTIN and saved coordinates are encrypted before they are stored, and are masked when shown back to you.
  • KYC and licence documents are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links. Every time staff view a seller's KYC details, it is recorded.
  • Connections between the apps and our servers are encrypted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data on the profile, farm and business-profile screens.
  • Withdraw consent for optional uses such as location and notifications from your phone or app settings.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account. We confirm it is you with a fresh SMS code.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer (below). You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

What deletion erases, and what stays

Erased: your profile, mobile number and photo; farms, crop cycles, soil and irrigation records; AI chats, voice sessions and crop scans; saved addresses and cart; seller bank and KYC details; the photos and documents stored with them.

Kept, but no longer linked to you: past orders and bookings, which tax law and the other party require, with your delivery address removed; and your listings, community posts, comments, reviews and the messages you sent to other users, which stay visible to them under the name "Deleted User".

Owner decision needed: whether messages, posts, comments, reviews and listing photos should instead be deleted with the account. The paragraph above describes what the app does today.
