Refund, Cancellation & Return Policy

Version {{version}} · Effective from {{effectiveDate}}

1. AgriStore orders

Cancelling

You can cancel an order yourself in the app (Account → My Orders) while it is still Pending, that is, before the seller has confirmed it. If you paid online, the full amount you paid is refunded to the same payment method.

Once the seller has confirmed your order, you cannot cancel it in the app; ask the seller or customer care. If the seller cancels it, or you refuse it at delivery and the seller records that, anything you paid online for those items is refunded automatically. If the whole order is cancelled, the delivery charge is refunded too.

Returns

You can return an item within 7 days of delivery only if it arrived damaged, defective, wrong or past its expiry date. Opened seeds, pesticides, fungicides, weedicides, fertilisers and growth promoters cannot be returned unless they were wrong or expired when they arrived. When the seller is at fault, the seller collects the item and you get back what you paid for it, including its delivery charge.

The app does not have a return button yet. To ask for a return, write to customer care at {{supportEmail}} within 7 days of delivery with your order number, the item and photos. We reply within 2 working days.

Cash on delivery

If you paid in cash and are owed a refund, it is sent to your UPI (Unified Payments Interface) ID or bank account within 7 working days after the return is accepted. Give these details to customer care. The seller, who received your cash, pays the refund, and we make sure it is paid.

2. Machinery and labour bookings

When online payment is available, you pay the full amount shown when you book. Your booking is then a request: it is confirmed only when the owner accepts it.

If the booking cannot be made because the machine or worker is already taken for those dates, the amount you paid is refunded automatically and in full.

You also get a full refund if (a) the owner rejects your request, (b) you cancel at least 24 hours before the booking starts, or (c) the owner does not turn up and customer care confirms it. If you cancel less than 24 hours before the start, or after it has started, the payment is not refunded.

To cancel a booking, or to claim a refund in cases (a) to (c), write to customer care at {{supportEmail}} with your booking details. We send these refunds within 2 working days of hearing from you.

3. Failed and duplicate payments

If money leaves your account but the order or booking is not created, or you are charged twice, the extra amount is returned to the same payment method.

4. How long a refund takes

Refunds for cancelled orders and failed payments start automatically. Refunds that customer care approves, for returns and rent bookings, are sent within 2 working days of approval; refunds for cash orders within 7 working days, as above. Once sent, a refund usually reaches your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or payment app.
