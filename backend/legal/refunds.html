1. AgriStore orders

Cancelling

You can cancel an order yourself in the app (Profile → My Orders) while it is still Pending, that is, before the seller has confirmed it. If you paid online, the full amount you paid is refunded to the same payment method.

Owner decision needed: what a buyer can do once the seller has confirmed or shipped the order.

Returns

Unless the product page says otherwise, a delivered item can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Some categories cannot be returned once delivered.

Owner decision needed: the list of non-returnable categories (for example opened seeds, pesticides and fertilisers), the condition an item must be in, who pays for return shipping, and whether the delivery charge is refunded.

Owner to note: buyers cannot yet request a return in the app. Until that is built, say here how to ask (for example by writing to customer care with the order number).

Cash on delivery

Owner decision needed: how a refund is paid for an order that was paid in cash.

2. Machinery and labour bookings

When you book, you pay the amount shown before you confirm. Your booking is then a request: it is confirmed only when the owner accepts it.

If the booking cannot be made because the machine or worker is already taken for those dates, the amount you paid is refunded automatically and in full.

Owner decision needed: what is refunded when (a) the owner rejects a paid request, (b) you cancel before the booking date, (c) you cancel on or after it, and (d) the owner does not turn up. Today the app does not refund the payment in cases (a) and (b), and renters have no cancel button. Decide the rule; the app then has to be changed to follow it.

3. Failed and duplicate payments

If money leaves your account but the order or booking is not created, or you are charged twice, the extra amount is returned to the same payment method.

4. How long a refund takes

We start a refund as soon as it is approved. It usually reaches your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or payment app.
