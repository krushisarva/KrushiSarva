Mobile apps

The KrushiSarva farmer app and the KrushiSarva Seller app do not use cookies. Your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Admin panel and web

When you sign in through a browser, we set two cookies. Both are strictly necessary: signing in does not work without them, so there is nothing to opt out of and no consent banner.

NamePurposeLifetimeReadable by scripts
rtKeeps you signed in (refresh token). Sent only to the sign-in endpoints.Until you sign out, or 7 days without useNo
csrfProtects your session from requests forged by other websites.Same as rtYes (it must be echoed back with each request)

What we do not use

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not let third parties set cookies through our apps or the admin panel. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and you will be asked before any non-essential cookie is set.
